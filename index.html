Know where you'll shelter.

Sources, refresh timing & privacy

This is an informational dashboard, not an emergency alerting system. Keep official emergency notifications and local broadcasts enabled. Current weather is modeled Open-Meteo data, not a nearby station measurement. U.S. alerts come from NWS. International weather works, but international official warnings are not integrated. News uses GDELT, with a clearly labeled fallback if available. Headlines and automated topic labels do not establish the severity or location of an incident.

The screen clock updates every second and wall-view headlines rotate every 8 seconds. Upstream news and weather are checked every 15 minutes; U.S. alerts every 2 minutes while the tab is visible. Source publication times differ from check times. Browsers may pause background tabs. No app can guarantee second-by-second emergency coverage. The Doomsday Clock is a dated, manually verified symbolic setting.

Search text goes to Open-Meteo geocoding (GeoNames). Selected coordinates go to Open-Meteo weather and, for U.S. locations, NWS. “Use my location” asks your browser, rounds coordinates to 0.01 degrees (about 1 km) and uses the same services; reverse geocoding, when available, uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Location stays in tab memory unless you enable “Remember on this device”; that choice saves the selected city and rounded coordinates in your browser's local storage. “Clear location” removes the saved location. No account, analytics or server-side location history is maintained by this site.
