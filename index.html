    How these comparisons are sourced

    The 2024 national platforms, current party agenda pages and explicitly dated 2026 leadership reporting establish stated positions, not verified results. Agency records, legislation, courts and independent analysis establish the record. Forecasts and interpretive tradeoffs are labeled, not presented as observed outcomes.

    Historical policies from 2021–2026 are dated. Economic and social trends usually have several causes; shared and inherited policies are identified. This is a curated, manually reviewed snapshot, not a live fact-check of every candidate or claim. No party ratings, targeted persuasion or voting recommendations.

    Abortion law and tariff references provide national policy context, not current individualized medical, legal or financial advice. Use official sources for rules that may have changed.

    01 / SEVERE WEATHER

    Know where you'll shelter.

    Follow local warnings and evacuation orders. For a tornado, choose a low, interior room away from windows. Never drive through floodwater.

    02 / POWER DISRUPTION

    Keep the basics working.

    Use flashlights, keep phones charged and plan for medical-device power. Keep generators outdoors, more than 20 feet from doors, windows and vents.

    03 / CIVIL UNREST

    Distance. Calm. A safe route.

    Sources, refresh timing & privacy

    This is an informational dashboard, not an emergency alerting system. Keep official emergency notifications and local broadcasts enabled. Current weather is modeled Open-Meteo data, not a nearby station measurement. U.S. alerts come from NWS. International weather works, but international official warnings are not integrated. News combines official NPR, BBC World, Guardian, Fox News and NBCNews.com RSS feeds through rss2json for personal, non-commercial use. Publisher selection is balanced, not a ranking of truth or importance. Only linked headlines and publication times are displayed; publishers retain all rights. Headlines and automated topic labels do not establish the severity or location of an incident.

    The screen clock updates every second and wall-view headlines rotate every 8 seconds. Upstream news and weather are checked every 15 minutes; U.S. alerts every 2 minutes while the tab is visible. Source publication times differ from check times. Browsers may pause background tabs. No app can guarantee second-by-second emergency coverage. The Doomsday Clock is a dated, manually verified symbolic setting.

    Search text goes to Open-Meteo geocoding (GeoNames). Selected coordinates go to Open-Meteo weather and air quality and, for U.S. locations, NWS. “Use my location” asks your browser, rounds coordinates to 0.01 degrees (about 1 km) and uses the same services; reverse geocoding, when available, uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Location stays in tab memory unless you enable “Remember on this device”; that choice saves the selected city and rounded coordinates in your browser's local storage. “Clear location” removes the remembered active location, but not your saved-city list. “Save city” explicitly saves up to 8 cities and rounded coordinates on this device; use each city’s × button to remove it. Saved cities are not shared between users or devices. No location is sent to news providers. Radar images come directly from NWS; choosing an external service link opens that provider’s website. Air quality is a CAMS model estimate, checked every 30 minutes; it is not a smoke detector or nearby monitoring station. Dallas report aggregates are checked hourly for a daily dataset, using citywide counts only, never device coordinates, exact addresses or personal details. Other crime coverage is explicitly unavailable or linked to official sources. Display preferences are stored on this device and can be reset in Display tools. “What changed today?” compares this tab’s observed headline links only and resets on reload; it cannot reconstruct earlier events. Sharing a city creates a named-city search link, not a precise-location link. Copying never posts or sends automatically. No account, analytics or server-side location history is maintained by this site.
